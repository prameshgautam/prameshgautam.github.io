About Me

I am Pramesh Gautam, a PhD Candidate and Research Assistant in Wireless Communications. My research centers on reliable and adaptive communication strategies for challenging wireless environments, particularly in industrial and non-terrestrial settings.

I am especially interested in how predictive methods, distributed optimization, and realistic simulation frameworks can improve system robustness and support the demands of future wireless networks.
