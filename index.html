About Me

I am Pramesh Gautam, a PhD Candidate and Research Assistant in Wireless Communications. My research centers on designing robust, reliable, and intelligent communication strategies for future wireless systems, especially in industrial and non-terrestrial scenarios.

I am particularly interested in connecting advanced signal processing, optimization, and realistic simulation environments to solve practical problems in modern wireless networks.

Research Interests

  • 6G wireless communications
  • Non-terrestrial networks
  • Reliability-aware SINR prediction
  • Link adaptation
  • Distributed beamforming
  • Industrial subnetworks and HRLLC
  • Distributionally robust optimization

Publications

  • Conference Paper P. Gautam et al., “RASP: Reliability-Aware SINR Prediction for Realistic Industrial Subnetworks,” IEEE ICC 2026.

Projects

  • Reliability-aware link adaptation for industrial subnetworks Exploring adaptive transmission strategies for dependable communication in realistic industrial settings.
  • Distributionally robust cooperative LEO satellite beamforming Investigating optimization methods for uncertainty-aware cooperative beamforming in non-terrestrial networks.
  • Sionna/Sionna-RT based wireless simulation frameworks Building reusable simulation pipelines for evaluating advanced wireless communication algorithms.
